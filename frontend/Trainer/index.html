Welcome Trainer 💪

The Trainer Panel serves as a dedicated workspace where trainers manage members, assign workouts, and monitor progress in real time. It enables structured planning, performance tracking, and direct engagement with assigned users. With clear visibility of member plans and training status, trainers can maintain discipline, accountability, and personalized support. Built for efficiency and control, this dashboard enhances both trainer productivity and member transformation.
